| Experiment or failure mode | A TT slot published its 64-bit payload separately from a plain 16-bit key, so an SMP reader could accept an old key with a new score/bound payload. |
| Disposition | **CLOSED 2026-09-07: the defect is real, BOTH repairs were rejected on measured cost, and the incoherence is now an ACCEPTED, RECORDED risk.** The exposed consumers are `src/search.cpp:1589`, which returns `tt_score` as the node value on a depth-and-bound match with no verification, and `src/search.cpp:1967`, which multicuts on a singular beta derived from it; worst case is a foreign mate score reaching the root. The pre-repair comment claiming a mismatched pair was "harmless ... bounds validated" was false; `src/tt.h` and `DESIGN.md` s3 now record the risk instead. Repair 1, `key16 ^ fold16(payload)`: The tag bound the pair correctly -- torn-pair tests passed, the old scheme failed their negative control, 25 collision-stress repetitions covered 10M contested writes, ASan/UBSan suites passed and the 1T bench stayed exactly 12,568,898 -- but it cost **-3.91% NPS at 1T with identical node counts** (6 strictly alternating `bench 13` repetitions per arm on the frozen gate binaries; means 3,666,005 vs 3,522,690; the two arms' ranges do not overlap). At BAS-P01's ~2 Elo per 1% NPS that predicts ~-7.8 Elo, and the registered 4T SPRT read `-11.30 +/- 9.64` (nElo `-17.87 +/- 15.23`, LLR -1.27, 2,020 games, zero forfeits, maintainer-stopped). Cause: `probe_copy` can no longer reject a slot on a 2-byte compare -- it must load the 8-byte payload and fold it for every scanned slot, with the fold on the branch's dependency chain. **The 4T reading is separately unusable as a verdict**: BAS-M02 and `tools/sprt.ps1` require fixed-N 10k at 4T and a per-thread-count `-Mode calibrate` null, `-use-affinity` is dropped at Threads>1, and no 4T null exists in `tools/results/`; the 1T NPS isolate is what condemns the implementation. H0 does not license restoring incoherent reads. Repair 2, IMPLEMENTED then REVERTED, `2bf43fb` (`analysis/tt_publication_coherence_v1.md`): `key16|score16|eval16|depth8|flag_age8` packed into the single atomic 64-bit word (exactly 64 bits, none spare) with `move16` moved to the separate 16-bit field, making the tear unrepresentable for every bound-deciding field. Bench exactly 12,568,898 on both arms; release and ASan/UBSan CTest 12/12. **Its throughput prediction was FALSIFIED.** Frozen prediction was pooled NPS in `[-0.5%, +1.5%]`, central `+0.3%`; measured **-1.22%, 95% CI [-1.72%, -0.76%]**, A faster in 1/16 rounds, per-build medians agreeing to 0.03%/0.14% within each arm (`tools/nps_ab.ps1`, 2 pooled PGO builds per arm, 16 alternating rounds, identical fingerprints). At BAS-P01's ratio that is ~-2.4 Elo. The frozen packet named this exact failure mode and range in advance -- the baseline's three 2-byte key loads reject more cheaply than three 8-byte word loads -- so the mechanism was predicted even though the headline was not. Caveat: the instrument's self-pair read `+0.33%` against a `0.30%` tolerance (CI `[-0.27%, +1.05%]`), marginally failing, which is small against the 2.7 percentage points separating the two candidate encodings but not against the acceptance band. **Maintainer decision: revert to plain-key and accept the risk.** 64 bits cannot hold key+score+eval+depth+flag+move (80 bits needed), so coherence is not purchasable at 10-byte density; ~2.4 Elo is a quarter of the entire BAS-P01 speed wave, the defect has never cost a measured game, and Stockfish ships this same tolerance in this same structure. No game gate was run: a `[-5,0]` SPRT on a true ~-2.4 sits between the hypotheses and cannot terminate. |
| Conditional lesson / coverage | Search must validate a shared record as one logical publication before consuming its bound -- the exposed consumers are the direct TT cutoff at `src/search.cpp:1589` and the singular multicut at `src/search.cpp:1967`, both of which return a TT-derived score. Exact 1T identity cannot exercise the race, but it does not license skipping a 1T THROUGHPUT isolate: a bench-identical correctness repair can still be a pure speed regression, and the games only looked like a coherence verdict. Measure NPS at identical nodes before attributing a game result to the mechanism -- BOTH candidates here were bench-identical and BOTH were pure speed regressions. A correctness repair has a price, and that price is evidence: when it exceeds the measured harm, the honest close is an accepted, precisely documented risk, not a repair shipped because repairs feel safer. Record the accepted risk where the next reader will hit it -- the code comment that carried the false claim -- not only in the ledger. |
