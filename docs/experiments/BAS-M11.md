| Result / disposition | **Registered, not yet run.** **Crash rule (maintainer amendment before the first game, 2026-10-05):** crashes are the maintainer's judgement. The result stands while crashes are few, of the order of 1–3 in 2,000 games, and are not concentrated enough to move the reading; every crash is listed with its game, engine, colour and exit code, and the reading is reported both with and without the crashed games. A Basilisk crash does not void the run; it is a correctness defect and opens its own repair leaf, with the crash's game, position and exit code. **Reading:** the verdict is Basilisk's performance against the five fixed ratings only; if the app's performance column includes the unfixed Rarog 2.5.0, the five-opponent figure is computed from the PGN instead.
