# BAS-C14

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | BAS-C14 |
| Experiment or failure mode | **Displayed-score normalisation (PLAN A.8.19, moved from D.3.3; Rarog's D.3 research card).** `info … score cp` prints Basilisk's internal units. Stockfish and Reckless print a win-probability scale, where `cp 100` is a 50% chance of winning. **Fit** (`tools/diag/winrate_fit.py`, Stockfish's `win_rate_params` model; output `tools/results/a819-winrate-fit/`): 1,160,575 scored positions from BAS-S20's 8,000 self-play games at `3+0.03`. The 50% point is **135** internal units at material 58 and **334** at material ≤ 17. Cubics in m/58: a = (−722.92, 2154.62, −2045.22, 748.46), b = (−8.63, 166.53, −222.08, 148.06), highest power first. So raw `cp` overstates by about 35% in the middlegame and 3.3× in the endgame. |
| Disposition | **NO_CHANGE by maintainer decision (2026-10-08).** `cp` stays in internal units. 29 tools parse the displayed score as internal units; the most important is B.0's scale-ratio instrument (`tools/diag/scale_ratio.py`, BAS-D20/D21), which B.2's evaluation-unit seeding depends on. Rescaling the display would change their meaning silently. `UCI_ShowWDL`, which would give win chances without touching `cp`, was offered and deferred by the maintainer: he will return to it if he needs it. The tablebase band display (A.8.14) and known-win evaluations below it are unaffected. |
| Conditional lesson / coverage | A display convention is a contract with every consumer of the output: list the consumers before choosing a mapping. This card came in on the donor's practice, with no measured Basilisk defect behind it. **Retry trigger:** every Basilisk instrument reads scores through a raw-score option or an internal channel, so a normalised display cannot leak into a measurement; or a user-facing need for comparable `cp` (score-based adjudication, a GUI report). The fit above is reusable then, refreshed on the current head. |
