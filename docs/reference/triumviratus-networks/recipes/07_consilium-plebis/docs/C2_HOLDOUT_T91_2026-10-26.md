# Consilium Plebis: loss on the t91 holdout against Consilium (24-26/10/2026)

The training loss of the two runs cannot be compared (different lambda schedules, data filters and batch). Here every
network is evaluated on the same positions of a file that neither run trained on, at fixed lambda, with the loss of
the trainer. Charts: [`../charts/c1_c2_holdout.png`](../charts/c1_c2_holdout.png),
[`../charts/c2_t91_by_expert.png`](../charts/c2_t91_by_expert.png),
[`../charts/c2_t91_by_bucket.png`](../charts/c2_t91_by_bucket.png). Scripts and raw outputs:
[`../analysis/holdout_t91/`](../analysis/holdout_t91/).

## Method

- **Data.** `t91_sample.binpack` (470 MB, from `jshriver/t91-binpacks`, Leela's test91 run). The P data are the
  BT4-relabelled Leela and Stockfish corpus ([`../DATA.md`](../DATA.md)); t91 is not in it.
- **Loss.** `c2_val_loss_fixed2.py`: batches of 8192 positions with the run's skip configuration
  (`random_fen_skipping=10`, quiet filters), the trainer's `calculate_sf_loss` with its default loss parameters, at
  lambda 1.0 (search evaluation only), 0.75 (the training target of P) and 0 (game result only). Values x1e-3.
- **Same positions.** Every network reads the same positions in the same order, Consilium included: the score tensors
  of the Consilium and Consilium Plebis streams are equal batch by batch, and the 120-batch values of epoch 246 of 24/10
  were repeated exactly on 26/10. The differences between networks are therefore paired: the error of a difference is
  computed over the per-batch differences (`t91_paired.py`, `t91_bucket.py`, `t91_expert.py`), about 0.13% of the loss
  over 300 batches against 0.25% for each single mean.
- **Windows loader.** The stream creation fails at random in some processes (access violation, every retry inside the
  same process fails too) and works in others, even with an idle CPU. `t91_run.ps1` runs one process per network and
  starts it again until it works. 20 threads per socket, one per core.
- **Consilium.** The 8.0 release network (`nn-consilium.nnue`). The value measured on the VM on 21/10 (5.240 at lambda
  0.75) came from another sample and is not used.

300 batches (2,457,600 positions), paired: Consilium 4.3276 / 5.2241 / 18.0549; ep246 -0.90 / -0.03 / +1.03%;
ep376 -2.08 / -1.67 / -0.32%; ep435 -3.44 / -2.88 / -0.83% (+-0.13% at lambda 0.75).

- Consilium Plebis passed Consilium at lambda 0.75 around epoch 240. The evaluation (lambda 1.0) led first; the
  prediction of the game result (lambda 0) caught up only after epoch 246.
- The gain accelerated as the LR fell: -0.013% per epoch from ep246 to ep376 (LR 64% to 27% of the peak), -0.020% per
  epoch from ep376 to ep435 (27% to 13%), while the slope of the training loss stayed the same. A straight line on the
  recent pace gives about -4% at lambda 0.75 for the end of P (epoch 549); fits that saturate give -2%, a straight line
  over all points -5%.

(ep376 -> ep435: change of the loss in % of the ep376 loss.)

- **No expert is short of data.** The experts with the fewest positions (e3, e0, e1: 3-8% of the training) are the
  ones furthest ahead of Consilium.
- **Every expert still learns at low LR**, by 0.9-1.7% in 59 epochs; the heavy-material experts e5-e7 bring 73% of the
  change and tripled their lead over Consilium. The middlegame, where the games are decided, is where the network
  gains now: more training at low LR is the lever, not a different share of positions per expert.

## Against Elo

Elo against Consilium at fixed 100k nodes (author's tests, same 8.1 search with the adopted per-expert levers): the
exports from ep358 to ep435 together +4.7 +- 3.5 over 10,418 games, ep463 +10.8 +- 5.5 over 4,033. From ep166, ep210
and ep246 (lambda 0.75 +2.7%, +0.3%, -0.1%; Elo -19, -11.5, -5) the exchange is about 4-5 Elo per 1% of t91 loss, with
-5 Elo at equal loss.

## About t91 as training data

`jshriver/t91-binpacks` (about 160 GB compressed, November 2025 to June 2026) holds the positions of Leela's test91, a
debug run that trains BT4 in reinforcement-learning mode with 800 visits per move (test90: 400). Its labels are the
search results of the run's own networks of the moment, not the BT4 relabelling of the P data, and they were never
measured against it (`../training/download_bt4.sh` excludes t91 by default for this reason). If it is ever used for
training, a month must be held out as the new holdout and its label quality checked first.
