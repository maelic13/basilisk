# Consilium Plebis: evaluation scale against Consilium (22-26/10/2026)

How wide the evaluations of the P exports are compared with Consilium's release network, on the same positions, and
how the scale moves with the Elo of the same exports. Chart: [`../charts/scale_trend.png`](../charts/scale_trend.png)
(41 exports, ep19-ep435); loss charts: `../charts/c2_loss_*.png`. Data and scripts: [`../analysis/scale/`](../analysis/scale/).

**Method.** 49,594 positions from the ep19 match games (every second ply from ply 12). Consilium Plebis exports
through the match engine `c2ep19.exe` with `setoption EvalFile` (ep19 reproduces the original run byte for byte; a
permuted export evaluates byte for byte like the plain one), Consilium from the run of 21/10 (`tm105r.exe`).
`scale_c2.py` on the search evaluation ("final"), positions with |eval| <= 1500 on both sides. Scale = sd(C2) /
sd(C1); the OLS slope of C2 on C1 and the inverse slope of C1 on C2 bracket it. Offset = C2 minus C1 for the side to
move, engine units (mean |eval| about 320). Lambda and LR from the logged global step of each epoch (the two resumes
of 21/10 put the step counter two epochs ahead: lambda reaches 0.75 at the end of epoch 167). Elo: the 100k-node
matches against Consilium (`match_moe/sprt_sprt_c2epNN_vs_tm105r_10_01_n100k_n0/n1.pgn`, both sockets), 95% interval
over game pairs.

## Reading

- **Scale.** Fast rise from ep19 to ep62 (0.79 to 0.92), slow rise to ep137 (0.945), then a plateau: 0.958 +-0.007
  over the 18 exports from ep166 to ep342, while the LR fell from 84% to 36% of its peak. Exports a few epochs apart
  differ by up to 0.02, so a single export is not a trend.
- **A small rise at low LR.** The seven exports from ep358 to ep435 (LR 32% to 13%) average 0.966 +-0.004, all at
  0.959 or above: 0.009 over the plateau, about 1.4 times its spread. The gap to Consilium is now about 3%,
  and the upper end of the bracket reaches 1.00-1.01. The rise sits in the experts of low and middle material (e0
  +0.015, e1 +0.013, e3 +0.016) and in buckets b0-b3 (about +0.01 each); e6, e7 and buckets b4-b7 are flat.
- **Elo with a constant scale.** From ep166 (-19) to ep269-342 (about -3 on average) the network gained about 15 Elo
  with the scale unchanged: the gain is in accuracy, not in width. The scale test of 23/10 agrees (section below):
  bringing the scale to Consilium's is worth nothing measurable at 5k, 60k or 100k nodes.
- **The smallest material keeps widening.** The bucket with at most 4 pieces rose from 0.82 (ep107-137) to 0.88
  (ep358-435), at every block.
- **Correlation and offset.** r with Consilium saturates at 0.965, unchanged to ep435. The side-to-move offset
  appeared between ep62 and ep87 and stays at about +12 on average (+12.1 over ep166-342, +12.9 over ep358-435),
  jumping between +4 and +19 from one export to the next: snapshot noise, no trend.
- **Train loss.** It rose while lambda fell (0.00316 at ep62 to 0.00353 at ep168) and falls since lambda is fixed:
  0.00313 at ep435, -11.2% in 267 epochs, at a nearly constant slope (-0.0011 to -0.0019e-3 per epoch; -0.0011 from
  ep406 to ep435, LR 20% to 13%).
- **Engine.** Nothing to change now. The test below finds a shared factor worth nothing measurable: it stays 0 and
  goes into the SPSA on the final network. With the gap now at about 3%, the starting point for that factor would be
  about 36 (x1.035) instead of 64.

## Test of 23/10/2026: the scale brought to Consilium's

C2EvalExp0..7 = 64 (x 1.0625 = 1/0.94 on both network heads) against 0, same dev2 build:
- ep189 against Consilium at fixed 100k nodes: -11.4 +- 8.6 over 1,582 with the factor, -14.5 +- 7.8 over 2,010
  without (release PGO c2ep19).
- Direct A/B on ep189 at fixed 60k nodes: -0.5 +- 8.2 over 2,040.
- Direct A/B on ep194 at fixed 5k nodes: +1.4 +- 5.3 over 8,474.

Close to zero at every node count (author: "almost irrelevant"): the factor stays 0. It goes into the SPSA on the final
network as one factor shared by the eight experts, starting from 64.
