# B.6.7 — the literal screen, the relevance read, the rails and the curvature sweep, then the joint search SPSA

**Status: registration frozen 2026-11-13, before any relevance read, sweep point or
game. Results are appended below the line.** Leaf: PLAN B.6.7 (class `V`). Ledger:
BAS-S61. Subject: the B.6.6 head, every default standing (B.6.6 adopted no donor form),
`bench 13` 3,783,135, with the literal screen of `src/kernel_literals.h` (the screen
commit `bebf2ad`: release code instruction-identical to the head's). The literal list and each
literal's donor line are `analysis/b67_kernel_literals.md`.

## 1. What runs, in order

1. **The wire** (B-105): every subject moved each way must change the `bench 13`
   replay over UCI. Literals at the read's points (§3): done before this registration,
   115 of 118 move it, the other three are read where they reach (the list's
   *Liveness*). The head's X coordinates one generator step each way, run with the
   read below.
2. **The relevance read** (B-102, §3): every subject at two points, zero games.
3. **The surface** (§4): the X coordinates and the literals that pass.
4. **Rails and boundaries** (B-103, B-104, B-64, §5): ranges, then the room report.
5. **The curvature sweep** (§6): WAC at four points about each surface coordinate;
   a flat one is dropped.
6. **The tune** (§7): one rule 7c run, one night, maintainer-run.

Steps 1 to 5 are agent-run and zero-game on an idle host; each records its exit
statuses and the binary's hash. Nothing in them is a verdict on strength.

## 3. The relevance read

- **Binary.** The screen build at `bebf2ad`: Tune flavour, `LITERAL_SCREEN`
  on, by `tools/build_test.ps1`, its manifest clean, hashed before any point. Node-
  and depth-limited counts do not depend on speed.
- **Points.** Each subject at `v − δ` and `v + δ`, `δ = max(1, round(0.2·|v|))`,
  inside its option's range (an X coordinate's at its bound when `v ± δ` is outside
  it). One side only for the three guard literals: `LitHindDownDepth` 3,
  `LitFhRedMin` 4, `LitFhRedBy` 2. `LitBoundCont` above reads 36000, which the bound
  holds at 32767 (+9.2%).
- **Read.** `tools/diag/run_suite.py` on `suite_v2` (106 positions), depth 14,
  stride 1, Hash 64, `Diag` on, the one option set and every other at its default.
  The reference is the same binary at its defaults.
- **Null points** (the chaos floor): four nudges of one unit, below anything a search
  decision resolves, so what moves is the tree's chaos and not a mechanism:
  `LitLmrBase` 698 (1/1024 of a ply on every reduction), `LitLmrBias` 2180,
  `LitCorrPawnW` 15342, `LitBoundCapture` 10693.
- **Rows.** For each row the per-position share is the counter over its
  denominator; the row's statistic is the median share over the positions where the
  reference's denominator is at least 1,000 (a position whose point denominator is 0
  is left out of both), and its change is the point's median over the reference's,
  less 1 (B.5.4's form).
  - *Firing rows* (threshold 10%): B.5.4's eighteen — LMR applied, LMR re-searches,
    TT move absent, count-skip nodes, quiet futility, capture futility, capture SEE,
    continuation pruning, RFP cuts, razoring cuts, ply-2 correction updates, null
    tries, ProbCut tries, singular tries, IIR reductions, quiescence futility,
    stand-pat cuts, quiescence fail highs — with quiet SEE / moves tested,
    hindsight deepenings / interior, hindsight shallowings / interior, TT cutoffs /
    interior, null cuts / null tries, singular double extensions / singular tries,
    multi-cuts / singular tries, negative extensions / interior, deeper re-searches /
    LMR re-searches, shallower re-searches / LMR re-searches, first-move fail highs /
    fail highs, and aspiration fail lows and fail highs / aspiration windows.
  - *Scale rows* (threshold 5%): reduction plies / LMR applied (the mean reduction),
    the fail-high index sum / fail highs (the mean index of the cutting move), and
    nodes (interior plus quiescence) per position, its median over the positions
    where the reference searched at least 1,000.
- **Eligibility.** A row decides only when every null point moves it by less than
  half its threshold (under 5% for a firing row, under 2.5% for a scale row). An
  ineligible row is reported with its null movement and decides nothing.
- **Rule.** A subject **passes** when, at either of its points, an eligible firing
  row moves by 10% or more or an eligible scale row by more than 5%. It fails
  otherwise, and the list records the largest eligible movement it made.
- **What passing means.** The subject changes what the search does at a move the
  SPSA can make, which is all B-102 asks. It is not evidence of a gradient.

## 4. The surface

- **Passing X coordinates** stay on it, seeded at the values the head runs. A failing
  one leaves it and is reported (an inert coordinate is B.8's).
- **Passing literals** join it as their screen options, `Lit<Name>`, on the screen
  build, which is the tune's binary: the tune build advertises both tables. A release
  build keeps reading the literal as a constant, so the tune costs no speed; theta's
  literals are baked by editing their values in `src/kernel_literals.h`. A joining
  literal's range is its option's, half to twice its value, except where §5 floors it.
- **A literal that fails** stays a literal, and the list says so with its read.
- **`tools/generate_spsa_surface.py`** gains a surface that is a named subset of the
  head's X lines and the kernel literals (it writes whole tables only now; B.5.8 left
  it for this preparation), with its test.

## 5. Rails, boundaries and room

- **B-103, rails.** For each surface coordinate the room report is read before the
  sweep. A range whose bound sits under two steps from the seed is widened so the
  seed has two steps each side, where the chess meaning allows: never past a value
  that disables the mechanism, never below depth 1, never past a signed 16-bit
  history entry. `CoreCorrUpdateScale`'s maximum goes from 30 to 64 (B.6.4 §6: the
  donor's value in this engine's units is 42.6, 63.8 without a best move, and every
  fit has moved it up). Each change is listed with its reason.
- **B-64, switch boundaries.** A continuous coordinate whose range reaches a value
  that switches its mechanism off is floored one unit above it.
  `CoreResearchShallower` (0 turns the shallower re-search off) is floored at 1 if it
  passes. The three guard literals, if they pass, keep their safe side only:
  `LitHindDownDepth` at least 2, `LitFhRedMin` at least 3 and `LitFhRedBy` at most
  3 (a node above depth `LitFhRedMin` loses `LitFhRedBy` plies and must keep one).
- **B-104, clips.** The rule 7c generator grades each perturbation by its room so no
  first arm leaves its range; a coordinate left with under one step of room is named.
- Range changes are tune-build metadata: no default moves and `bench 13` stays
  3,783,135 on the release and the tune builds.

## 6. The curvature sweep

- **Points.** Each surface coordinate at `v − 2δ`, `v − δ`, `v + δ`, `v + 2δ` (§3's δ,
  inside the §5 range) beside the defaults.
- **Per point:** WAC solved at 100,000 nodes (`fixed_budget_probe.py`, all 300
  records read).
- **Classification** (Rarog's frozen rule, B.4.6's and B.5.4's), on the WAC column
  `w`: *flat* when `max(w) − min(w) ≤ 2`; otherwise *curved* when the maximum is at
  interior points only; otherwise *monotone*.
- **Rule.** A flat coordinate is dropped from the surface and reported; curved and
  monotone stay. A flat or monotone surface does not skip the tune (PLAN B.6).
- **Read beside, decides nothing** (PLAN B.6.7's prior, RAR-S54: Rarog's
  selectivity read +4.06 ± 3.71 a uniform 15% toward less pruning): for each
  surface coordinate that sets a pruning or reduction margin, whether its WAC
  maximum lies on the less-pruning side of the seed.

## Amendment 2 (2026-11-13, before any game; a review by a second session, at the maintainer's request)

It changes the surface's rule, the screen and the tune's binary. It changes no
game condition, no schedule and no seed, and no game has been played. The
relevance read and the sweep stay recorded above as they ran; their predictions
are scored there and are not re-scored.

### Why

- **The relevance read's verdicts do not separate live from inert.** Recomputed
  from `points/` (`tools/results/b67-20261113/review/noise.py`): over all 381
  points, the row *continuation pruning / moves tested* has a robust spread
  (1.4826 × MAD) of **6.6%** and passes its own 10% threshold at **24.7%** of
  the points; *IIR reductions / interior* 4.1% and 5.8%. Of the 102 passes,
  **81** pass on the continuation row and **73 pass on nothing but those two
  rows**. The three null points that moved read that row at 2.9%, 4.1% and 4.3%,
  just under the 5% that kept it eligible. So a subject passed mostly when a
  chaotic row happened to move, whatever the subject was: `LitStatCont2` passes
  and `LitStatCont1` fails; `LitContW2`, `W4`, `W6` pass and `W1`, `W3`, `W5`
  fail; `CoreLmrTtPvBase` (3,105 thousandths of a ply at every PV-near node)
  fails. A fail there is not evidence of an inert coordinate, and a pass is not
  evidence of a live one.
- **A coordinate without a gradient costs the others nothing.** By
  `analysis/b56_spsa_method_2026-11-12.md` §3 the standard error of a
  coordinate's `Elo(+c) − Elo(−c)` over 2,000 iterations is 0.86 to 0.95 Elo
  whatever the surface holds, and a coordinate with no gradient walks about a
  third of a step. Leaving a live coordinate off costs its whole gain. The
  surface is therefore chosen by what a tune can resolve, not by which chaotic
  row moved.
- **Rule 7c's rail clause can only stop a tune on a coordinate seeded at a
  bound.** `CoreResearchShallower` (seed 1, floor 1) and `LitHindDownDepth`
  (seed 2, floor 2) could move one way only; an outward gradient past the
  familywise line would end the night at 2,000 with no theta.
- **The screen build's arithmetic was 32-bit at run-time values.** The release
  build proves the continuation update's width at the compiled literals
  (B.6.5). The screen build read `LitContW*`, `LitContConsistencyScale` and the
  fail-low parent's literals at run time under the same 32-bit product, which
  is at 97.6% of `INT_MAX` at the defaults (B.6.4 §4; 87% seen in play): one
  perturbation up on two of them passes it.
- **The move picker was outside the screen.** PLAN named the node kernel,
  quiescence, the history updates and the root record; the picker's ordering
  weights are search literals of the same kind and were on neither table of the
  list.
