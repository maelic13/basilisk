## Findings

### 1. No rail, no clamp, no range hiding a value (range)

- **Evidence.** Final positions run from 0.10 to 0.85 of their ranges; no arm was
  clamped on any iteration; no rail or rail candidate at 2,000.
- **Reading.** Amendment 2's two narrowed ranges and the B-103 rails held.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 2. The singular margin falls for the third fit running (convergence, cohesion)

- **Evidence.** 252 (B.3's seed) → 232 (B.3's fit) → 201 (B.5.4) → **160**, the
  run's strongest gradient (z −3.82, the one coordinate over the line, then −2.17).
  At depth 10 the singular bound moves from 31.9 below the TT score to 25.4. The
  double and triple margins barely moved (`B3SingTripleTtPv` −0.77 steps, the rest
  under a step).
- **Reading.** Still a way-point, now with the whole search refitted around it. The
  direction is again toward the donor's much smaller margin. The extension budget
  and the double and triple margins did not follow, so the extension family is not
  being refitted as one.
- **Fix.** None to the bake. Read the singular family's form, not only its margin,
  before the next search fit. **Owner:** C.10's classical-stage fit.

### 3. The history updates reweight toward the malus (cohesion)

- **Evidence.** The quiet-move malus grows on every term it has: `LitMalusShare`
  +1.61 steps (1159 → 1335), `CoreMalusSlope` +1.02, `CoreMalusDecay` +1.04,
  `CoreMalusCap` +0.70. The stat score trusts the main history less
  (`LitStatMain` −1.48, 2252 → 1940) while LMR reads the stat score more
  (`CoreLmrStatScale` +0.69). `CoreMalusSlope` reverses Cluster 1's fit, which took
  the donor's 968 to 829 (`analysis/b23_constants_2026-10-14.md`); it is back at 931.
- **Reading.** Coherent, not cancelling: failed quiets are punished harder and
  sooner, and the reduction leans on continuation history over main history.
  Cluster 1's slope move was a block fit against a fixed malus share the joint fit
  could finally reach.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none; Phase H's history re-read if the NNUE eval moves
  the move-ordering picture.

### 4. Reductions deepen while extensions grow; the tree grows by 13% (interaction, unit)

- **Evidence.** `CoreLmrTableScale` +1.37 steps (more reduction everywhere), with
  `LitLmrAlphaHi` −1.25 (less extra reduction when the estimate sits far below
  alpha) and `LitLmrWindowPv` +0.62. The singular margin's fall adds extensions.
  The `bench 13` replay grows by 13.1%. The WAC sweep had read `CoreLmrTableScale`
  best at −40% (270 against 259).
- **Reading.** The fit traded reduction for extension at equal time: deeper cuts on
  ordinary moves, more plies on singular ones. As in B.3.3 and B.4.6, the fit went
  against the sweep's WAC maximum, and WAC is not Elo. A larger tree at equal Elo
  would be a cost; the gate reads it at equal time.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** B.6's SPRT.

### 5. Two coordinates keep drifting one way across fits (cohesion, convergence)

- **Evidence.** `CoreCorrUpdateScale` 12 → 14 → 16 → 17 → 18 → **20** (B-103's
  widening to 64 let it move freely; the donor's value in this engine's units is
  42.6, 63.8 without a best move). `B3IirMinDepth` 5 → 6 → 7 → **8** (z +1.86 in the
  last window): IIR starts a ply later at each fit.
- **Reading.** A joint fit was meant to settle these. One more small step each,
  rather than a jump, says the gradient is shallow near the seed or that the
  correction's update and IIR are held by something outside the surface.
- **Fix.** None now. **Owner:** H.6's fair re-reads (the correction keys, the kings
  in the non-pawn key, are its rows) and C.10.

### 6. The literals moved, but mostly by noise (B-100)

- **Evidence.** 93 of 101 literals' rounded centres left their seeds, median 0.30
  steps; four moved a full step (`LitMalusShare`, `LitStatMain`, `LitLmrAlphaHi`,
  `LitEvalDiffPawn`). Every history bound moved, by at most 0.84 steps.
- **Reading.** Movement is not Elo: most of these values are the donor's plus
  noise. Theta is the registered estimate and is baked as it stands, but a literal
  that moved half a step tells nothing about where it wants to be.
- **Fix.** None to the bake. B.11.3's constants census must not read "differs from
  the donor's value" as "fitted": the list marks each literal's seed and theta with
  its steps. **Owner:** B.11.3.

### 7. A defect the bake found: a scale literal could not be baked (implementation)

- **Evidence.** `KLIT_SCALED` divided by the literal's compiled default and
  compiled to the identity in release builds. The tune build applied
  `LitLmrHistDivScale`, `LitCorrNoMoveScale` and the picker's seven scales against
  1024 correctly, but the baked release read every scale as its own default, a
  ratio of 1: the first baked build read `bench 13` 4,021,446 against theta's
  replay of 4,278,307. Every advertised default matched theta, which located it.
- **Reading.** No game played the defect (at the head every scale was 1024, where
  both forms agree), and the tune's theta is valid. The fingerprint check against
  the replay is what caught it.
- **Fix.** `0f30bf1`: a scale literal is in 1/1024 in every build. The baked
  release now reads 4,278,307, the replay. **Owner:** done.

### 8. The forgotten-mate rule lost its end-to-end witness (test coverage)

- **Evidence.** `test_b5_root`'s fixture proved a mate in 8 at depth 15 and read
  2607 at depth 16, which the rule kept. The fitted tree proves it at depth 17 and
  keeps it to depth 26 with the rule switched off (a local build), and no WAC mate
  is lost to depth 18 with the rule off.
- **Reading.** The rule's predicate keeps its unit checks; its rescue in the root
  loop (the rotation and the restored line) has no witness on this tree. The
  fixture is re-based to the proof's lines.
- **Fix.** Find a witness on a larger mate corpus with the rule off and restore the
  end-to-end check. **Owner:** B.9's correctness pass.
