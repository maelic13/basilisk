## 3. The blocker: 242 frozen slots, stranded by the re-level

`fit.py` derives the corpus's surface with `surface.py rank` on 30,000
train rows (`--rank-positions`) and freezes every free linear slot under
200 rows of support (`--min-support`, C-52). BAS-O12 ran the tuner on the
full manifest with no support freeze. So its linear stage had 1,025 free
slots, where run 1's had 783: **242 slots were held at the seed.**

The held slots, by table:
- PSTs, 204 of them:
  - mg king 28, eg king 23;
  - knight 25 + 25;
  - queen 21 + 21;
  - bishop 20 + 21;
  - rook 7 + 8;
  - pawn 3 + 2.
- Queen mobility 22, other mobility 8, and 8 single terms.

**Why it is a defect, not just a tighter fit.** A PST level is not
identified on its own. It trades against the imbalance's piece-times-pawn
products (BAS-O12 Correction 2, HANDBOOK §6). With material held, the fit
re-levels whole tables, but a frozen entry does not move with its table.

| Table | Free entries' mean shift, seed → A | Frozen entries |
|---|---:|---|
| mg pawn | −215 | e7, f7, g7 |
| mg rook | −65 | h2, g4, g5, g6, f8, g8, h8 |
| eg rook | +52 | the same squares and h4 |
| eg queen | +33 | 21 squares |
| mg knight | −17 | 25 squares |
| eg knight | +19 | 25 squares |

A frozen entry's shift is 0 by construction. So, for example:
- The seed scores a middlegame pawn on f6 and on f7 alike (+13 and +10).
- Run 1 scores them −102 and +10: a 112 cp step from the table alone.
- A middlegame rook on f8, g8 or h8 keeps its seed value while the rest
  of its table drops 65 cp. In the endgame the rest rises 52 cp.

These steps are artifacts of the freeze, not chess.

The king tables and the eight mobility tables are mean-anchored, so their
frozen entries are not stranded the same way.

**The support itself is not low.** 200 rows in 30,000 is a 0.67%
occurrence threshold, not "the data cannot see it". Scaled to the
3,547,892 train rows, the 242 slots have a median of about 12,000 rows
each. Only 4 would fall under 200 at full scale: queen mobility 0 and 1,
mg and eg, with no support at all in the sample.

C.2.1 built this rule knowingly: PLAN records "269 slots freeze" on its
own 30,000-row check. What it did not foresee is the interaction with a
free PST level. The registration missed it because its numbers came from
BAS-O12, which had no freeze.

**Options for the amendment (R2):**
1. **Count support on the whole train split, or scale the threshold to
   the sample** (200 × 30,000 / 3,547,892 ≈ 2 rows). This frees 238 of the
   242 slots and matches the surface BAS-O12 measured. Add a guard that
   fails a fit when a support-frozen entry sits in a table whose level is
   free. A small change to `fit.py`, or to `surface.py`'s rank.
2. **Hold frozen entries relative to their table's level.** The freeze is
   kept and the stranding goes away. This is a tuner change that adds
   machinery for four slots.
3. **Anchor the PST levels**, as the king tables are. This fixes the gauge
   against the imbalance cross terms instead. It changes C-19's gauge
   structure and the surface's contract; that is research of its own.

**My position: option 1, with the guard.** It restores the surface the
registration meant, at the cost of a re-run of about an hour for both
fits. Everything below is from run 1 and describes it. The re-run's
report repeats each read.
