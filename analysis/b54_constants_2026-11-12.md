## Findings

### 1. No rail, no clamp (range)

- **Evidence.** No final centre sits at an edge. No perturbation crossed a bound in
  2,000 iterations: the smallest gap is `B5AspBase`'s 0.29 steps, early, near its
  lower bound of 500 while its centre was near the seed. `B3IirMinDepth`'s 0.50 is its
  narrow range (3–10) against a perturbation of about two.
- **Reading.** No range hides a wanted value, and no gradient was bent by a clamp.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 2. The window widened and then stopped; it is a time-management dial (convergence, interaction)

- **Evidence.** `B5AspBase` moved +0.50 and +0.59 steps in the first two thirds and
  +0.03 in the last (z +0.24). The window's three alone shrink the tree by 11.0%.
  `B5AspMsqDiv` and `B5AspGrowth` read no signal; their centres are noise around the
  seed. BAS-S54's audit table puts 2202 beside its 2115 column: the fail-high stop
  ends about 26% of searches, as at the seed, and the time manager's effort shares
  barely move.
- **Reading.** The base found its value inside the block, short of A3's 2820. The
  width's time-management effect is not what moved: the stop at 2× and the effort scale
  stand where BAS-S55 (c) read them, and BAS-S53 *CONDITIONS* sends nothing back.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 3. The singular margin falls for the second fit running, and was still moving (convergence, cohesion)

- **Evidence.** 252 (B.3's sweep seed) → 232 (B.3's fit) → 201 here, with z −4.06, the
  block's strongest gradient, and −2.03 in its last third on half the first third's
  reach. The singular bound is `tt_score − margin · depth / 63`, so at depth 10 it moves
  from 36.8 cp below the TT score to 31.9, and more TT moves are found singular and
  extended. The arm raised the singular tries' share by +39.4% by the median (the
  joiner read), and the fit extends further still rather than back.
- **Reading.** Theta is a way-point for this coordinate, not a converged constant. The
  direction is toward the donor's much smaller margin (B.3's read-only point). Whether
  the extension budget and the double and triple margins should move with it is a joint
  question.
- **Fix.** None to the bake (theta takes the rounded centres). **Owner:** B.6.7's joint
  surface, which must hold the singular family together; B.5.6 reads it as a coordinate
  whose block ended while it moved.

### 4. The null move's entry tightens for the third fit running (cohesion)

- **Evidence.** B.3's fit made the bare null move less aggressive on all four moving
  terms; B.4.6 took `B3NmpPerPly` 16 → 13 and `B3NmpBase` 163 → 172; this fit takes it
  13 → 11 (z −2.50). The entry is `static_eval ≥ beta − per_ply · depth − improving ·
  imp + base`; with the arm's base of 172 the first depth at which the null move may run
  with static eval below beta moves from 14 to 16.
- **Reading.** Three independent fits, each with other parts of the search moving, push
  the same term the same way. Either the null move's form is too aggressive on this
  engine at every depth, or something it relies on (the verification, the reduction)
  is mis-set and the entry compensates.
- **Fix.** Read the null move's form, not only its entry constants, before the next fit.
  **Owner:** B.6.4's audit, then B.6.7's surface.

### 5. Two coordinates keep drifting one way across fits; the correction scale woke late (cohesion, convergence)

- **Evidence.** `CoreCorrUpdateScale` 12 → 14 → 16 (B.2.3's two blocks), 17 by Cluster 2,
  18 here; it was idle in the first third (z −0.09) and moved in the other two (+1.90,
  +1.64). `B3IirMinDepth` 5 → 6 (B.4.6) → 7 here, so IIR now starts a ply later.
- **Reading.** Small steps in one direction per fit are what coordinate-block fitting
  produces when the joint optimum lies further out. Not a defect; evidence for B.6.7.
- **Fix.** None now. **Owner:** B.6.7's surface; B.5.6 for the late start.

### 6. The joiners carry the tree's growth (unit, interaction)

- **Evidence.** The five joiners alone grow `bench 13` by 16.2% and theta by 17.0%:
  more singular extensions, IIR a ply later and a stricter null move all add nodes. The
  window alone saves 11.0%.
- **Reading.** A larger tree at equal Elo would be a cost; here theta reads +10.8 in
  games at equal time, so the growth is paid for. NPS is not read and need not be: both
  arms of BAS-S56 ran the same binary.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 7. Which coordinate carries the +10.8 is not read (attribution)

- **Evidence.** BAS-S56 reads the vector. No per-coordinate game read exists, and none
  is registered.
- **Reading.** Findings 3 and 4 name the likeliest carriers; the window's direct signal
  stays small (BAS-S53's width run, −1.0 ± 9.1).
- **Fix.** None; a split would chase single digits (Phase H). **Owner:** none.
