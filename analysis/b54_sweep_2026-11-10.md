## The joiner read (§5.8 as §A2.5 amends it)

- **Read:** `tools/diag/run_suite.py --depth 14 --stride 1 --hash 64` on `suite_v2`
  (106 positions) with `Diag` on, the arm at its defaults and the OFF arm at its
  defaults. These are the sweep's default point and its OFF reference.
- **Share:** for each row, a mechanism's counter over its denominator per position. The
  row's firing share on each arm is the median over the positions where the OFF arm's
  denominator is at least 1,000; a position where the arm's denominator is 0 is left out
  of both arms and counted. The change is the arm's median over the OFF arm's, less 1.
  The total share (sums over all 106) is reported beside it.
- **Rule:** a row joins when its median moves by 10% of itself or more. A row that
  moves by 10% in total and not by the median is listed with the five positions that
  carry most of the total's change and does not join. A joining row admits the
  coordinates it names, each seeded from the value the arm runs.
- **The rows** (the mechanism-to-counter map of B.3.2's and B.4.5's reads, with
  §5.8's named coordinates and the rows the implementation record flagged):

## The sweep

- **Points** (§A1.5, in the new unit):
  - `B5AspBase` at 1000, 1410 (the defaults), 2000, 2820, 4230, 5640, 9870, 19000 and
    30000, growth 47.
  - `B5AspGrowth` at 32, 64 and 96 at the seed base, and again at the cheapest base:
    the base point with the lowest depth-14 median against the OFF arm, ties broken
    by the lower depth-14 total. If the cheapest base is the seed, the second line is
    the first and is not run again.
  - `B5AspMsqDiv` is on the surface and not swept (packet §11 BAS-S54). Nothing else
    is swept; the engine clamps silently, and no point here is outside its range.
- **Per point:**
  - `bench 13` by `bench_replay.py` (`bench`'s 40 positions in order over UCI, one
    `ucinewgame`, Hash 16, one thread). At the defaults it must reproduce 3,920,652,
    and the OFF arm 4,144,790, before any point is read.
  - WAC solved at 100,000 nodes (`fixed_budget_probe.py nodes 100000 src/wac.epd`).
  - `suite_v2` at depths 14 and 17 (`run_suite.py`, `Diag` on, Hash 64, stride 1).
    Nodes are interior plus quiescence nodes per position. Read against the OFF arm's
    same run: the total over the total, and the median over positions of the
    per-position ratio. Recorded, with re-searches per window and the failed share.
- **Classification, per swept coordinate, on the WAC column `w`** (Rarog's frozen
  rule, B.4.6's):
  1. *flat* when `max(w) − min(w) ≤ 2`;
  2. otherwise *curved* when the maximum of `w` occurs only at interior points and at
     neither edge;
  3. otherwise *monotone*, the maximum reached at an edge.

  The base classifies on its nine points (edges 1000 and 30000); the growth on the seed
  base's line, 32, 47, 64 and 96 (edges 32 and 96). The cheapest base's growth line is
  recorded and not classified.
- **Rule.** A swept coordinate read flat is dropped from the surface and reported;
  curved and monotone stay. Flat or monotone on both does not skip the tune (this
  packet's BAS-S54). Node columns do not classify: the width run read 19000 at −1.0 ±
  9.1 against the node read's prior (BAS-S53, A5's falsifier fired), so nodes choose
  only the cheapest base's growth line.
