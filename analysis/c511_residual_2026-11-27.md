## 1. Questions

PLAN's four, for the whole leaf:

1. How much of the same-search deficit (217.3 on 1.11.0) is left, and in
   which family and phase?
2. Is what is left evaluation at all: speed (C.5.3), the search's margins
   on the new evaluation (C.7), or positions a search visits and a game
   does not (the corpus)?
3. Which donor forms were screened out and are worth a read in games,
   threats first?
4. What information of Basilisk's own can a concept residual screen still
   name?

This stage answers the second first, because the answer decides what the
others are worth. If most of the residual is throughput, a family
ablation attributes a remainder too small for it to resolve, and the
next cluster is the speed pass, not an evaluation form.

### 2.2 Speed

- **Evidence.** In BAS-M27 Stockfish 16 HCE searched 2.79M nodes a second
  and 192,355 nodes a move; the head 2.01M and 137,013. Stockfish's clock
  bought 1.388 times the nodes. Mean depth without mate scores was 18.24
  against 18.17.
- **Evidence.** In BAS-O16, one search and one binary, the donor's
  evaluation ran 2.86M nodes a second and the rebuilt one 2.53M: 1.129
  times. The bridge is 2.07% of the oracle's time (BAS-O07). The rest is
  the evaluation's own cost against the donor's, for the same forms.
- **The ledger's conversion** (PROCESS, *Converting NPS to Elo*): about
  2 Elo per 1% of nodes a second at `3+0.03`, measured to +22% in
  self-play, about 145 Elo a doubling; the textbook rule is 60.
- **Inference.** Taken at face value the frontier's 1.388 is 0.47 of a
  doubling: 28 Elo by the textbook rule and 69 by the measured one,
  against a deficit of 39.8. The oracle's 1.129 is 0.175 of a doubling:
  10 to 25 of BAS-O16's +42.4. Neither conversion was measured across
  engines or in the donor's search, and Stockfish's node count may not be
  Basilisk's (C.5.3's first question). So this is a reason to measure,
  not a result.

## 3. Hypotheses

- **H1, throughput.** Most of the frontier deficit, and a third to a half
  of the same-search read, is nodes a second. Then C.5.3 leads, and no
  evaluation cluster opens on the meters alone.
- **H2, evaluation quality.** At equal nodes the donor's evaluation still
  leads the head's by more than 10. Then a matched ablation can say where
  (§8).
- **H3, the search's margins on the new evaluation.** Basilisk's margins
  were fitted on the old evaluation; C.7 refits them. This stage does not
  separate H3. It bounds it: H3 lives in what the frontier pair keeps at
  the odds.
- **H4, positions off the game line.** BAS-O10's read, on the old
  evaluation, is read beside the results (§7).

  first arm's rating less 1500 is E.
- **`e0`, equal time.** `3+0.03`, seed 511001, a two-engine gauntlet of
  1,500 cycles, zero permitted faults. BAS-O08's, BAS-O14's and
  BAS-O16's conditions, so E(0) compares with 217.3, 146.2 and 42.4.
- **`e0n`, equal nodes.** The same pair, seed and openings. Both arms
  carry `nodestime=1000`:
  - Stockfish's own option. The search then counts nodes where it counted
    milliseconds: at a game's first move the clock becomes a node
    account, and the account alone decides every later move
    (`timeman.cpp` and `search.cpp:261` at the `oracle/hybrid` tag).
  - The clock is `8+0.08` (`tools/colosseum/gauntlet-node-clock.toml`),

    one 7.6M (BAS-O16's nodes a second). The games take as long as at
    `3+0.03`.
  - The wall clock stays the referee. An arm is safe while it runs at
    least 1,000 nodes a millisecond over any stretch from a game's
    start. In BAS-O16's 3,000 games the slower arm's slowest such
    stretch ran 1,050; its median game's slowest ran 1,867.
- What the pair says: `e0n` is the two evaluations' quality at equal
  nodes. `e0` less `e0n` is what the head's evaluation loses in this
  search by being slower, the bridge's 2% included.

## 6. Decision rules, frozen

With D the deficit, minus `frontier`, and S the gain, `odds` less
`frontier`:

1. **Speed at the frontier.**
   - S at or above two thirds of D: speed leads. The recommendation to
     the maintainer is the speed research (C.5.3) ahead of any new
     evaluation cluster.
   - S at or below one third of D: speed does not lead. The clusters are
     chosen on evaluation and search evidence, and C.5.3 keeps its place.
   - Between: both, ordered by rule 2.
   - D inside its own interval of zero is the leaf's stop rule: the
     family work closes.
2. **Evaluation quality in one search.**
   - `e0n`'s 95% lower bound above +10: the second stage (§8) is
     registered and prepared.
   - Otherwise no ablation is played. Under about 10 Elo the instrument
     cannot attribute (PROCESS, *Matched ablation*, rule 5). The donor
     forms still open (threats, the Rarog note's candidates C and D) go
     to their own reads at the search's leaves, or to Phase H.
3. **`e0` less `e0n`** is handed to C.5.3 as the evaluation's share of
   the speed gap in one search. It decides nothing here.
4. **Flags.** A flagged read decides nothing alone:
   - `e0n` with the arms' nodes a move more than 3% apart, or with time
     losses above the wrapper's ceiling;
   - `odds` with the arms' nodes a move more than 7% apart. S is then
     read per percent of nodes a move actually equalised.
5. **Clamped draws.** Rule 2 is applied to `e0n` with drawn bare-king
   wins rescored. Both figures are reported.

Stop rule and order:
- One run per read at 3,000 games, never extended or replaced. An
  interrupted read resumes from its checkpoint.
- Order: `e0`, `e0n`, `frontier`, `odds`. 12,000 games, a little over
  three hours.
- Intervals are computed as if the reads were independent. Each pair
  shares its openings, which the difference's interval does not credit.
