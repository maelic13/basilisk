## Findings

### 1. No rail, no clamp, no reversal inside the block (range)

- **Evidence.**
  - No centre ended closer than 22% of its range to an edge, or came closer than 20%
    at any iteration.
  - No perturbation left its range: on all 15 coordinates the centre plus or minus
    that iteration's scale stayed inside all 2,000 times, so no gradient was bent by a
    clamp.
  - No coordinate went out and came back by a full step. The largest such excursion
    is `B4QsStandPatBlend`, down to 416.93 (−0.50 steps) and back to 437.
- **Reading.** No range hides a wanted value. The edges the sweep's WAC column pointed
  at, `CoreRfpPerPly` 198 and `B4QsFailHighBlend` 256, were not approached.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 2. `B3NmpPerPly` is the one clear signal, and it had not settled when the block ended (convergence)

- **Evidence.**
  - z −4.08 over the block, −3.13 over its later half. Across 15 coordinates that is
    about p = 0.0007 after correction.
  - Its lowest centre, 13.44, is within 0.05 of its final one, so it ended at about
    its extreme.
  - The result gap between its plus and minus sides did not shrink: about −3.2 ± 1.2
    Elo in the first 1,000 iterations and −4.0 ± 1.2 in the second (one standard
    error), or roughly 1.5 to 2 Elo per unit. A coordinate nearing its optimum would
    show that gap falling.
  - The centre is 13.49, so the rounding takes it to 13, 0.01 from rounding to 14. The
    raw movement is −1.25 steps, so it counts as a full step either way.
- **Reading.** The optimum is probably below 13, inside the range (2–32). The stop rule
  counts coordinates and ends the tune, and one coordinate does not justify a 12-hour
  block. It does mean this value is a way-point, not a converged constant.
- **Fix.** None to the bake: theta takes the rounded centres, never a selected value,
  and the rule stands. At that slope what is left is likely a few Elo at most, so it
  waits for the next fit of the null move.
- **Owner:** B.6, with finding 3.

### 3. The null move's entry tightens again, on the only null-move terms on the surface (cohesion)

- **Evidence.** The entry condition is `static_eval ≥ beta − per_ply·d − improving·imp
  + base` (`search_kernel_core.cpp`). Both moving terms tighten it, and the change
  grows with depth. Before the improving term, static eval must clear beta by:

  | Depth | 3 | 5 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 20 |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | Seed (163, 16) | 115 | 83 | 35 | 3 | −29 | −61 | −93 | −157 |
  | Theta (172, 13) | 133 | 107 | 68 | 42 | 16 | −10 | −36 | −88 |
  | Tighter by | 18 | 24 | 33 | 39 | 45 | 51 | 57 | 69 |

  - The first depth at which the null move may run with static eval below beta moves
    from 11 to 14.
  - `B3NmpBase` has now moved the same way twice: 151 → 163 at B.3.3 and 163 → 172
    here (z +2.08).
  - B.3.3's finding 1 read the same direction on all four of its moving null-move
    terms, and B.3.4 then read the eval gate at −6.1 ± 9.7 on the fitted constants, so
    the gate stays 0 and there is no stack to price.
  - B.4.5's depth-14 read had the arm moving `B3NmpPerPly`'s population by +16.4%,
    which is why it was admitted.
- **Reading.** Across two fits the games keep asking for a less aggressive bare null
  move. The arm moved this population up and the fit tightens entry, which is the
  direction that would take it back, but no count was read at theta and the cause is
  not established. The surface let the fit pay only through entry: `B3NmpImproving`,
  `B3NmpRedBase` and `B3NmpRedEvalDiv`, which B.3.3 moved, are fixed here. How much of
  the move belongs to the reduction instead is not known.
- **Fix.** None to the bake. All five null-move terms that have moved belong in one
  joint fit.
- **Owner:** B.6 (its joint tune), carried in the departures table with finding 2.

### 4. `B3IirMinDepth` undoes B.3.3 (reversal across fits, not clear)

- **Evidence.** 6 → 5 at B.3.3 (centre 5.38), 5 → 6 here (centre 6.16; z +1.82). The
  rule reads it as +0.50 steps because the step floor is 2, but one unit is the real
  change: IIR stops firing at depth 5. Alone it grows `bench 13` by 4.2%. B.3.3's
  finding 7 read 5 as more IIR, the direction of B.3.2's IIR-off read.
- **Reading.** A coordinate crossing the same rounding boundary in opposite directions
  on two trees reads more like flat between 5 and 6 than a preference. The store at 1
  is why this coordinate is on the surface, but no read connects the two.
- **Fix.** None: theta takes 6, and the gate prices it with the rest.
- **Owner:** none (recorded for B.6's reader).

### 5. Reverse futility widens per ply, reversing B.3.3's small move (reversal across fits, weak)

- **Evidence.**
  - `CoreRfpPerPly` 101 → 99 at B.3.3, 99 → 107 here. z +2.20 over the block, but +0.93
    over the later half, so the signal is early.
  - `CoreRfpImproving` 98 → 94 (z −1.60) also widens the margin, on improving nodes.
  - `CoreRfpCorrMult` 538 → 498 → 457 narrows it where the correction is large (z −0.98).
  - The three alone shrink `bench 13` by 10.3%, against what a mostly wider margin
    predicts.
- **Reading.** Two z values near 2 among 15 coordinates are about what chance gives
  (0.7 expected), so this is not evidence of a real slope. The bench sign is the
  column's chaos (S3: a single step moved it 43% in the sweep), not a defect in the
  margin.
- **Fix.** None. **Owner:** none.

### 6. The quiescence coordinates did not move (the hypothesis, mostly against)

- **Evidence.** `B4QsFutBase` 86 → 82 (z −1.19), `B4QsStandPatBlend` 441 → 437 (z
  +0.08), `B4QsFailHighBlend` 462 → 466 (z +0.33). None reached half a step at theta.
  The blends wandered by up to 0.50 and 0.22 steps and came back.
- **Reading.** BAS-S51's hypothesis said the unfitted conversions and the margins
  fitted on the old quiescence would move toward each other. They did not: the three
  coordinates this cluster added read flat around their seeds at STC, which gives no
  reason to doubt the unit conversions. The sweep's curved lines gave no clear gradient
  in games. Its curvature did its registered job, the go/no-go, as B.3.3's finding 3
  says it should.
- **Owner:** none (recorded).

### 7. The surface is mostly flat at STC (the competing explanation, mostly)

- **Evidence.** One of 15 coordinates moved a full step and one z is clearly outside
  chance. Eight of theta's 11 changes have |z| below 2.
- **Reading.** BAS-S51's competing explanation holds for all but the null move. Theta
  bakes those eight by the rule, because the estimator is the centre, not a selection.
  Whether the fit is worth anything is BAS-S52 (b)'s question. The null move is the
  likeliest source of any gain, and a null result would not surprise this analysis.
- **Owner:** B.4.7 (BAS-S52 (b)).

### 8. Theta shows no cost that binds, and WAC does not gate it (instrument)

- **Evidence.** Theta reads WAC 254 against 258, inside the sweep's 248–262 column;
  its tree is +3.5% and its paired depth −0.34, with 31 positions better and 33 worse.
- **Reading.** WAC at this budget does not read Elo (B.3.3's finding 3, and the sweep's
  note here). None of this is a problem. The floors at the bake are the check that
  binds, and they are separate.
- **Owner:** the bake.

### 9. The bake touches lines the OFF arm shares (structure)

- **Evidence.** Theta changes 11 defaults.
  - Three are Cluster 3's own lines (`B4QsFutBase`, `B4QsStandPatBlend`,
    `B4QsFailHighBlend`), which only the arm reads, so they change in place.
  - Eight are earlier clusters' lines that the OFF arm also runs: `CoreRazorMargin`,
    `CoreRfpPerPly`, `CoreRfpImproving`, `CoreRfpCorrMult`, `CoreLmrCutNodeNoTt`,
    `B3NmpBase`, `B3IirMinDepth`, `B3NmpPerPly`.
- **Fix.** The maintainer's B.3.3 decision applies: the eight go in
  `BASILISK_B4_CORE_DEFAULTS` (`src/search_params.h`), so the OFF arm and its
  fingerprint 6,090,091 stay put. That table runs after the B3 table, so its
  `CoreRfpPerPly` and `CoreRfpCorrMult` values override B.3.3's. If the bake trips
  `test_tt_switches_live`, its precondition is repaired in its own commit (B.4.4's
  review).
- **Owner:** B.4.6's bake.
