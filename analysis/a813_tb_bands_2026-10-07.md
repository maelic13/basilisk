# A.8.13 — Score bands and in-search tablebase probes

- State / class: `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` for A.8.14; gate A.8.15 designed below, `R2`
- Owner / date: agent, 2026-10-07
- Decision needed: none before implementation. A.8.15's gate is
  maintainer-run and registered before any game.

## Known evidence

**Evidence, the bands today** (source at `19204e7`):
- Mates occupy `|v| ≥ MATE_SCORE − MAX_PLY = 31,872` and are ply-adjusted in
  the hash table (`tt.h` `score_to_tt`/`score_from_tt`).
- A tablebase result is a flat `±tablebaseWinScore = ±20,000`
  (`search.cpp` `score_from_syzygy_wdl`; `constants.h`), with no distance from
  the root. `is_tablebase_decisive` reads `[19,872, 31,872)`.
- The static evaluation is clamped only to `±(MATE_SCORE − 1)` (`search.cpp`
  quiescence stand-pat and negamax `static_eval`). KBNK's override reaches
  27,420 by default (BAS-E28/E29) and 31,780 at the top of its tuning range.
  Without tables, KBNK prints `cp 22048` at depth 1 and so reads as
  "tablebase-decisive" (A.8 review item 3).

**Evidence, the in-search probe today.** `negamax` probes when the root is not
tablebase-ranked, the node is not the root, no move is excluded, and
`ply == 1 || depth ≥ SyzygyProbeDepth`. `Syzygy::can_probe_wdl` adds the piece
limit, no castling, and **rule-50 = 0 when `Syzygy50MoveRule` is on**. A result
returns at once and is stored as `TT_EXACT` at `depth` with no move.

**Clerical correction to the A.8 review.** Item 4 of
`analysis/rarog_parity_review_2026-10-06.md` and PLAN A.8.13's text say the
probe has "no zeroing-move condition". It has one under `Syzygy50MoveRule`,
the default. At the default probe depth 1 the depth condition is Stockfish's
too. What differs is the value, the bounds, the stored depth and the root
switch.

**Evidence, consumers that read "not a mate" as "not decisive".** Each tests
`|v| < MATE_SCORE − MAX_PLY`, so a tablebase value of 20,000 passes as an
ordinary score:
- the reverse-futility `eval` refinement from the hash table (`search.cpp`
  ~1813). Its own comment says a TB-range score must not drive it, but today
  one does.
- null-move's unproven-score clamp (~1875);
- ProbCut's `|beta|` guard (~1895);
- the move-loop pruning guard `best_score > −(MATE − MAX_PLY)` (~2026);
- singular extension's `|tt_score|` guard (~2159);
- **correction-history training** (~2505). With tables configured it trains
  on `best_score − static_eval ≈ 19,000` at every probed node.

The pool vote, the `go mate` stop, the mate-in-1 stop and the
aspiration-window skip test mates on purpose and stay as they are.

**Donor** (Stockfish `sf_19`, `edb0d9db`, `search.cpp` step 7 and `types.h`):
- `VALUE_TB = VALUE_MATE_IN_MAX_PLY − 1`; a probe returns `VALUE_TB − ply`.
- The band is `[VALUE_TB − MAX_PLY, VALUE_TB]`; evaluations stay below it.
- A win is a lower bound and a loss an upper bound; a draw (cursed and
  blessed under rule 50) is exact at `±2` toward the cursed side; with the
  rule off, a cursed win is a win and a blessed loss a loss.
- The node returns on an exact result or a cutoff, storing at
  `min(MAX_PLY − 1, depth + 6)` with no move. A PV node otherwise raises
  alpha (win) or caps its result (loss).
- `value_to_tt`/`value_from_tt` ply-adjust the whole decisive band.
- `is_decisive` guards the pruning and the correction update.

**Prior** (BAS-X35): Rarog's adoption measured **+11.4 ± 8.5** with tables.
Moving band values by ply in Rarog's hash table changed its bench *without*
tables, through window arithmetic; Rarog kept band values unadjusted.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (leading):** a decisive tablebase band next to the mate band, bound-
  correct probes and decisive-aware guards remove three things at once:
  - the eval/tablebase overlap;
  - correction history trained on tablebase values;
  - the indifference between short and long conversions, which a flat value
    cannot express.

  Without tables, nothing changes.
- **H2:** guard widening reaches the bench without tables through window
  arithmetic, as Rarog's table adjustment did. The bench decides (below).

- **Root:**

- **Display:** `format_info_line`.

## Implementation handoff (A.8.14, `I2`)

1. **Band.** In `constants.h`: `tablebaseValue = 32000 − 128 − 1 = 31,871`;
   a probed win is `tablebaseValue − ply`; the band is
   `[tablebaseValue − MAX_PLY, tablebaseValue] = [31,743, 31,871]`.
   - `is_tablebase_decisive` reads that band.
   - Add `is_decisive(v) = |v| ≥ 31,743`.
   - `static_assert` that the band sits directly below the mate band in
     `search.h` and `tt.h`.
2. **Evaluations stay below the band.** Clamp quiescence stand-pat and
   negamax `static_eval` to `±31,742` instead of `±(MATE_SCORE − 1)`.
   KBNK's default 27,420 is unaffected; its tuning range above 31,742 now
   clamps.
3. **Guards.** The six consumers listed above test `is_decisive`. The pool
   vote, `go mate`, the mate-in-1 stop and the aspiration skip stay mate-only.
4. **Probe** (in `negamax`, at the current site and under the current
   conditions):
   - value: win `tablebaseValue − ply`, loss `−(tablebaseValue − ply)`;
     cursed win `+2` and blessed loss `−2` under `Syzygy50MoveRule` (as
     today and as Stockfish), but a win or a loss with the rule off; draw `0`;
   - bound: win lower, loss upper, otherwise exact;
   - return on exact or cutoff, storing that bound at
     `min(MAX_PLY − 1, depth + 6)` with no move;
   - at a PV node, a lower bound raises `best_score` and `alpha`, and an
     upper bound caps the node's final `best_score`;
   - the `ply == 1` exception becomes Stockfish's
     `pieces < limit || depth ≥ probe depth`.
5. **Root switch.** In-search probes stay off when the root was ranked by
   DTZ, or by WDL and not winning. A WDL-only winning root keeps probing,
   since nothing else drives it forward (Stockfish's rule).
6. **Hash table.** `score_to_tt`/`score_from_tt` ply-adjust the tablebase band
   as well as the mate band. **If that changes `bench 13` without tables**
   (H2), store band values unadjusted instead, Rarog's form, and record which.
7. **Root values.** A clean win's root score is `tablebaseValue`, a loss
   `−tablebaseValue`, and the 1–49 cp display of A.8.8 is unchanged for
   spoiled results.
8. **Display.** A band value prints `cp ±(20000 − (tablebaseValue − |v|))`:
   a win in `n` plies of tablebase play reads `cp 20000 − n`.
9. **Tests:**
   - the band constants and `is_decisive`;
   - a fixture probe: a KQvK child returns `tablebaseValue − ply` as a lower
     bound;
   - KBNK without tables prints below the band (no tablebase display) and
     `is_tablebase_decisive` is false for it;
   - the display mapping;
   - the root's clean-win score;
   - the final extended line still extends at a tablebase root (the existing
     engine tests);
   - **exact bench 14,978,465 without tables**, and release and sanitizer
     CTest.

## Prospective prediction (frozen before any gate game)

- **Without tables:** bench exact, so no strength change. Confidence 0.75:
  H2 may move the bench through the guards or the table adjustment, and
  item 6's fallback contains the latter.
- **With tables on the standard book:** +3 Elo, 80% interval [−4, +10]. The
  correction-history fix and conversion preference are real but rare, since
  few UHO games reach ≤ 6 men with the search still deciding.
- **Conversion:** every tablebase-won root with a clean win is won; no time
  losses.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- A non-tablebase bench change that item 6's fallback does not remove: the
  guards reach the bench. Stop and re-derive before any game.
- A won tablebase root not converted, or a time loss with tables configured:
  an implementation defect.
- A gate result whose interval lies below 0: return to research.

## Gate for A.8.15 (maintainer-run; registered before any game)

1. **One Syzygy path,** byte-identical on both sides:
   `D:/chess/tablebases/syzygy3456`, 1,020 files, manifest hash as in
   `tools/diag/endgame_cohort_v1.manifest.json`. Default probe options.
   A run file `tools/colosseum/sprt-repair-tb.toml` extends `sprt-repair.toml`
   with `SyzygyPath` in both engines' options; the wrapper's dry run proves
   the option resolves and is advertised.
2. **Strength:** repair bracket `[−5, 5]` nElo on the standard UHO book with
   tables configured, at `10+0.1`. The longer clock is chosen because
   151 GB of tables against 128 GB of RAM makes cold reads likelier under
   concurrency. Concurrency 7, so each slot keeps two cores. Cap 4,000 pairs.
   Candidate: the A.8.14 head. Baseline: the head before it.
3. **Correctness counts** from the same games:
   - every game that reaches a tablebase-won root with a clean win under
     rule 50 is won;
   - zero time losses;
   - one `bestmove` per `go`;
   - legal PVs (the runner's own checks).
4. **Activation read:** from the PGNs' engine comments where available,
   otherwise a 200-position probe of 7–9-man positions taken from the games,
   searched at 100k nodes by both binaries: the share of searches with an
   in-search tablebase hit, per side. A zero share means the change was not
   exercised, and the strength result says nothing about it.
