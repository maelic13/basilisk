  The bench column is reported as the tree surface and does not enter the

**Decision by the frozen rule: four coordinates are curved, so B.2.3 runs
the registered SPSA blocks.** The surface is the core table's 50
coordinates, `tools/spsa_configs/colosseum/core.tune.toml`; none of the
swept five read flat, so none is dropped.

Readings that do not change the decision:
- **Every curve peaks at the seed itself.** The seed is the shared point:
  249, against 237–247 everywhere else. The WAC instrument therefore puts
  the seeds at or near a local optimum on all five lines.
  - At 100k nodes a single solve is about the instrument's resolution, so
    the curvature is real by the frozen rule but shallow.
  - What the SPSA can find is bounded accordingly: P2's +40 for the fitted
    cluster now rests mostly on the 45 unswept coordinates and on
    interactions.
- **BAS-S22's prediction, read.** The node-margin and move-loop groups
  curved as predicted (`CoreRfpPerPly`, `CoreLmpSquareScale`), but
  `CoreQuietFutBase` read monotone. `CoreLmrOffset` curved against "little
  on the LMR 1024ths terms": a miss on mechanism.
- **The bench column is not a smooth surface.** Single steps move the tree
  by −40% to +8%, and the defaults are the largest tree on three of five
  lines. That is why the rule reads the WAC column, as Rarog's sweep
  found.
- **Bench 39's share of each point's tree** (`points/bench39.txt`): 20.1%
  at the defaults (36.4% before `CoreLmpExemptChecks` moved to 0), and
  from 4.6% to 30.9% across the points. One position swings with any
  coordinate, so its share carries no single coordinate's signal. §24.1's
  `CoreCorrLimit` question stays a B.2.3 residual, unchanged.
