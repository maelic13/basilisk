## 1. What is decided, and what this packet fixes

**Decision (maintainer, 2026-11-25).** The evaluation is rebuilt as one
unit in classical Stockfish's form. Ideas and forms may come from the
donor; every value that ships is Basilisk's own; Basilisk's own forms
return where they give strength, each on its own read (C.3.6).

**Decision (the frozen rule of `analysis/c31_transplant_2026-11-25.md`
§7).** The two-family port is not taken. The transplant's gain read
−1.6 ± 12.4 Elo over 3,000 games with no fault (`t40-b`, BAS-O15), and
rule 2 names the whole rebuild when the gain is 40 or less. The two
remaining reads cannot change that; the control informs §11 only.

This packet fixes what the port is: the reference, the scope, the
order of computation, the units, the parity check, the delivery and the
reads that follow. Implementation owns the engineering inside it.

## 3. The four questions

1. **Mechanism.** The donor's families are fitted to one another: its
   king danger reads its own attack maps and mobility area, its passer
   terms assume tables that pay little for rank, and its winnable step
   and scale factor shape the whole endgame half. Strength comes from
   that set acting together on the positions a search visits. BAS-O14's
   non-additivity and BAS-O15's flat transplant are the evidence that
   the unit is the set.
2. **Interactions**, in this codebase:
   - `Evaluator::evaluate_full` (`src/eval.cpp`): the order of the sum
     and its three reads. The port has its own order (§6).
   - `apply_endgame`, `mate_drive`, `pawnless_scale`,
     `rook_ending_scale`, `ocb_draw_scale` (`src/eval_endgame.h`,
     `src/eval.h`): Basilisk's endgame layer. Kept, composed by §6.8.
   - `damp_rule50` and `Evaluator::evaluate` (`src/eval.h`): kept.
   - `LAZY_MARGIN` and the lazy audit (`src/eval.cpp`): replaced on the
     new arm by the donor's two exits (§6.7).
   - The pawn and imbalance caches (`Evaluator::pawn_table_`,
     `imbalance_table_`), and the ablation mask in the pawn key
     (`eval_ablation_key`, `src/eval_common.h`).
   - `EVAL_PARAM_LIST`, `EvalTrace`, `reconstruct` (`src/eval_params.h`,
     `src/eval.cpp`), `tools/texel/tuner.cpp`, `surface.py`,
     `bake.py`: the fit's model of the played score.
   - The search reads the static evaluation in centipawns through
     margins fitted to the head's scale
     (`src/search_kernel_core.cpp`); they stay frozen (C.10 owns them).
   - The oracle compiles `src/eval.cpp` as one file
     (`tools/oracle/build_oracle.ps1`).
3. **Invariants** (`docs/DESIGN.md` §3, and what the change touches):
   - The evaluation is a function of the position alone. No contempt,
     no root score. `tests`' cache-purity test holds on the new arm.
   - `evaluate_undamped` carries no halfmove clock; the TT stores it.
   - `apply_endgame` receives and returns White's point of view.
   - Every static score stays below the mate band, and below
     `KNOWN_WIN` unless the endgame layer set a tier.
   - A draw verdict is set only by the layer's rules, as today.
   - A scale factor from the general heuristics is never 0.
   - Per-node code allocates nothing and takes no lock.
   - The off arm reproduces `bench 13` = 3,359,329 at every commit.
4. **Falsifier.** Two, registered in §11: the seed read (the port at
   converted seed values against the head, BAS-M25) below +30 Elo
   returns the leaf to research before any fit; and parity (§9) is a
   hard gate that fails on one differing integer.

## 4. The reference: `9587eeeb`'s evaluation

**Decision proposed.** The port's reference is the classical evaluation
at Stockfish `9587eeeb` (`SF_classical`): `evaluate.cpp`, `pawns.cpp`,
`material.cpp`, `psqt.cpp` and what they read. C.0 chose `sf_16`'s as
the family donor on a default-keeping rule; for a whole port the
choice reopens, and these decide it:

- **Purity.** `sf_16`'s classical evaluation reads the search's root
  score in its lazy thresholds, has no tempo, and damps specialised
  endings through a wrapper shared with the network. `9587eeeb`'s is a
  function of the position once contempt is zero.
- **It is the one measured.** BAS-O14 and BAS-O15 read this
  evaluation, and the oracle that will meter the port carries it.
- **The parity instrument exists.** The pinned transplant oracle
  already prints this evaluation's families as exact integers, checked
  against its own trace (BAS-O15's check 4).
- **Nothing is lost in play.** The two are level within about 8 Elo in
  both searches (BAS-O09).

What `sf_16` has that this reference lacks (`DoubledEarly`,
`KingOnFile`, the two-phase imbalance, later retuning) becomes a list
of candidate terms for C.8, each on its own read. Counter-argument:
`sf_16`'s values had three more years of tuning. They are seeds here,
and the fit replaces them.

## 6. Exact semantics of the new arm

Inside the new arm every quantity is in Stockfish units until step
6.9. A value named a *seed* is the reference's constant.

1. **Start.** `score` = material and tables + imbalance. No contempt.
2. **Pawns.** `score` += the pawn structure score, White minus Black.
3. **First lazy exit.** If `|mg + eg| / 2 > L1 + npm / 64`, go to 6.6
   with what is summed. `L1` seeds at 1400; `npm` is both sides'
   non-pawn material in the reference's middlegame values.
4. **Pieces, mobility, king, passed.** In the reference's order, each
   White minus Black. The piece functions fill the attack maps the
   later families read.
5. **Second lazy exit**, as 6.3 with `L2` (seed 1300). Otherwise
   `score` += threats and space.
6. **Winnable.** The complexity adjustment moves both halves as the
   reference does. The strong side is White when the adjusted endgame
   half is above zero, Black otherwise.
7. **Lazy exits and the trace.** A traced build evaluates past both
   exits and records where a release build stops and what it serves,
   as today's checkpoint does.
8. **The endgame layer and the scale factor.** In this order:
   - Taper with the scale factor at 64 and convert (6.9). Call it `t`.
   - Run Basilisk's endgame layer on `t` as the off arm runs it today,
     without its opposite-bishop step: overrides, tiers, the pawnless
     scale, the rook-ending scales, the verdict.
   - If the layer overrode or scaled, its result stands.
   - Otherwise the value is the taper with the general scale-factor
     heuristics for the strong side, converted.
   So the layer takes the place of the reference's material-entry
   scale, and the general heuristics apply only where it is silent, as
   the reference applies them only where that entry is normal.
   The mate drive keeps its present gate and arithmetic, on Basilisk's
   own piece-count phase and the converted score.
9. **Taper and conversion.**
   `v = (mg * ph + eg * (128 - ph) * sf / 64) / 128`, in the
   reference's integer arithmetic, with `ph` its phase 0 to 128. Then
   `cp = v * S / 1024`, truncating toward zero. `S` is one fixed
   number: 628 (0.613) until the tuner's model exists, then set once by
   the rule of §7.
10. **Tempo and side.** The value turns to the side to move and the
    tempo is added, as the reference does, converted with the value.
    Where the layer overrode, no tempo is added, as today.
11. **Bounds.** A value the layer did not set is clamped inside
    `±(KNOWN_WIN − 1)`; a diagnostic build counts the clamps.
12. **Damping.** `evaluate_undamped` returns step 11's value;
    `evaluate` applies Basilisk's `damp_rule50`. The reference's own
    damping is not ported.

Integer arithmetic follows the reference wherever it truncates: the
taper, king danger's square over 4096 and its sixteenth, the
proximity terms, the imbalance's sixteenth.

## 7. Units and the conversion

- **Evidence.** 0.613 centipawns a unit by outcome (C.0).
- **Rule for `S`.** Once the tuner models the new arm, `S` is the
  integer for which the single K fitted on corpus A's validation rows
  at the seed is nearest the off arm's K on the same rows. Both Ks and
  `S` are recorded. `S` then never moves: it is a gauge, classed fixed
  in the surface.
- **Not done here:** separate middlegame and endgame conversions. The
  transplant used two (697 and 2022 over 1024), and the control read
  says whether that recalibration costs strength in the donor's
  search. The seed arm keeps the donor's own balance, because that is
  the number promised; the fit owns any recalibration (§11).

## 8. Values

- **Seed vector.** The reference's constants, exact, in units. It is a
  diagnostic arm and a parity instrument. It never ships: the flip
  commit's header holds the fitted vector.
- **Linear slots.** Every value the score is linear in is one registry
  slot. Where the reference multiplies two constants, the port holds a
  table whose entries equal the reference's products at the seed, so
  the slot is linear and parity stays exact. Where truncation makes
  that inexact, the reference's arithmetic is kept and its constants
  are knobs or structure constants. The record lists each choice.
- **Knobs.** King danger's weights, as today's king-safety knobs are.
- **Structure constants**, which no static fit prices: the phase
  limits and its piece weights, `L1` and `L2`, king danger's
  threshold and divisors, the complexity constants, the general
  scale-factor numbers, the space threshold, the mobility-area and
  outpost definitions. They are classed fixed with that reason, and
  C.9 owns making them Basilisk's own (§13).
- **Own-values audit**, at C.3.5's close: every slot whose shipped
  value equals its seed is listed with its class and reason.

## 9. Parity

**The reference producer.** A new oracle patch,
`tools/oracle/oracle-eval-reference.patch`, applied after the two
existing ones, adds a command that prints for the current position,
as exact integers in units and with contempt zero:
- the start (tables, imbalance), each of the seven families and the
  four piece types, both halves;
- the complexity adjustment, both halves after it, the phase, the
  strong side;
- the material entry's factor for the strong side and whether a
  scaling function gave it; the general heuristics' factor; the factor
  applied;
- the value before the tempo from White's side, the final value, the
  lazy exit taken (0, 1, 2), and whether a specialised evaluation
  answered.

It changes no value: the patched oracle's bench counts equal BAS-O15's
check 1 at all four configurations, and its final value equals the
unpatched `sf9587-stock`'s `evalraw` (`tools/donor/`) on every fixture
row.

**The fixture.** At least 10,000 game positions: the 12,119 rows
BAS-O15's checks used (every 21st of C.0's gate-game positions), with
the halfmove clock set to 0, plus hand-built positions until every
slot and every branch of §6 fires on at least 20 rows or is listed as
unreached with its reason. Its file and the producer's hash are
committed under `tests/fixtures/`.

**The test.** On the new arm at the seed vector:
- every family's two halves, the start, the complexity adjustment and
  the phase equal the reference's on every row a specialised
  evaluation did not answer;
- the lazy exit taken equals the reference's;
- on *layer-silent* rows (the reference's material entry is normal and
  no scaling function answered, and Basilisk's layer neither overrode,
  scaled nor drove), the general factor and the value before
  conversion equal the reference's;
- the layer-silent rows are at least 90% of the fixture;
- every value is colour symmetric (`tests/colour_mirror.h`);
- one changed seed fails it (checked once, by a deliberate break).

One differing integer fails the ticket. A departure the handoff does
not list is a return.

## Amendment 1 — seven points the handoff left open (2026-11-25)

Raised by implementation before any build; none is a false premise.
Each ruling is part of the contract from here on. No registered
prediction changes: BAS-M25's seed arm is the function as amended.

1. **Grain.** The reference rounds its value to a multiple of 16 before
   the side turn (`v = (v / 16) * 16`), which §6 did not list. The new
   arm has no grain. Reason: Basilisk's frozen search was fitted on an
   ungrained score, and a 16-unit step is about 10 cp. This is a listed
   departure, and a grain is a Phase H candidate (the reference's gain
   from it was small). The producer prints the value before the grain
   and the final value; parity is on the first, and §9's `evalraw`
   identity on the second.
2. **Tempo.** One arithmetic: `tempo_cp = Tempo * S / 1024`, truncated,
   added after the side turn on every path the layer did not override.
   The slot stays in units (seed 28). Parity is on the value before the
   tempo. §6.11's clamp follows it.
3. **The mate drive.** As proposed. Its gate reads `t` (the converted
   taper at factor 64, no tempo) with Basilisk's piece-count phase at 6
   or less and `|t|` above 200. Its endgame addition `D` is tapered by
   that phase, `D * (24 - phase) / 24`, and added to `t` before the
   layer runs. Where the layer then stays silent, the value is the
   converted taper at the general factor, plus the tapered `D`. A row
   the drive fires on is not layer-silent.
4. **Non-pawn material** is read everywhere in the reference's fixed
   middlegame values, never in the material slots: the phase, both lazy
   thresholds, the space threshold, the material tests of the general
   heuristics and of the complexity step. Those values are structure
   constants (§8). A test that is an equality against one piece's value
   may be written as the piece count it stands for.
5. **Chess960-only terms** (the cornered bishop) are not ported and are
   listed as unreached for that reason.
6. **Linear slots or knobs.**
   - Imbalance: the reference's arithmetic with its sixteenth is kept,
     and its 36 coefficients are knobs.
   - King danger: its weights are knobs, with the square over 4096 and
     the sixteenth kept, as §8 says.
   - Passed pawns are not knobs. §8's table rule applies, because it is
     exact here: the proximity terms and the `k * w` bonus become
     tables by rank and distance, or by rank and case, whose entries
     equal the reference's truncated products at the seed. They are
     free linear slots.
   - Ticket 7 reports the knob count and the descent's time on corpus
     A. If the knobs cannot be fitted in reasonable time, that is a
     return, not a redesign.
7. **Fixture coverage.** As proposed: ticket 0 commits the 12,119 rows
   and the hand-built rows that can be named now; each later ticket
   counts its own slots and branches on the new arm and appends rows
   where one is under 20. The fixture is append-only, every appended
   row's integers come from the same pinned producer, and its hash is
   recorded per ticket with the unreached slots and their reasons.

## Return 1 — the reference's imbalance is zero where it has a scaling function (2026-11-25, implementation)

Ticket 1 parity: on the rebuilt arm at the seed, psq and the phase equal
the reference on every one of the 11,659 rows a specialised evaluation did
not answer; the imbalance differs on 163.

**Evidence.** `Material::probe` (`material.cpp`) returns as soon as
`Endgames::probe<ScaleFactor>(key)` finds a registered scaling function,
before the imbalance and the pawnless factors are computed, so the entry's
imbalance stays 0 from its `memset`. The reference registers six:
KRPKR, KRPKB, KBPKB, KBPKN, KBPPKB, KRPPKRP. On the fixture these are 227
rows, and the reference's imbalance is 0 on every one of them. Our
polynomial is also 0 on 64 of them, which leaves the 163 that differ. The
other generic scaling assignments (KBPsK, KQKRPs, KPsK, KPKP) come after the
imbalance and leave it alone. The fixture's `scalefn` column says whether a
function *answered*, not whether one was registered, so it cannot pick out
these rows.

This is not a slot defect. It is material.cpp's use of its scaling functions,
which §5 says is not ported, and it is also part of the start, where §9
requires parity.

**Options.**
1. Reproduce it: the new arm's imbalance is 0 for those six material
   sets. Parity holds as written. Basilisk's endgame layer already scales
   KRPKR and KRPPKRP, so this keeps the donor's start in the endings the
   layer owns. The cost is a seventh rule about material sets, a donor
   artefact, to carry until C.4 (the former C.7) reads it.
2. Keep the polynomial there: a listed departure. The producer then prints
   whether a function was registered (a new column, appended without
