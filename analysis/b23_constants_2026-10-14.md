## Findings

### 1. The kept IIR is tuned nearly off: overlapping reductions for a missing TT move (design / cohesion)

- **Evidence.**
  - `CoreIirMinDepth` runs 4 → 8, the top of its 3–8 range. The rail may
    hide a wanted value above 8, i.e. off.
  - The legacy IIR (`search_kernel_core.cpp` 728) reduces every non-PV node,
    all nodes included, at depth ≥ 4 when there is no TT move or the entry
    is stale (`tt_depth < depth − 3`).
  - Inside Cluster 1 a missing TT move is already paid for three other ways:
    - hindsight (`depth−−` on a moderate prior reduction);
    - LMR's cut-node term `+933·!ttMove` (`CoreLmrCutNodeNoTt`);
    - Step 19's `+1127` for the first move without a TT move.
  - The donor's IIR is narrower: `!followPV && !allNode && depth ≥ 6 &&
    !ttMove`, with no all-node or stale-entry trigger.
- **Reading.** The cluster stacks a fourth "no TT move" reduction on top of
  the donor's three. The tune removes the one that does not belong to the
  donor's population.
- **Fix.** B.3 (already scoped "IIR against hindsight") replaces the legacy
  IIR with the donor's form (depth ≥ 6, PV and cut nodes, no TT move only).
  It settles the IIR-off arm as a categorical run against it. Until then the
  bake takes the tune's value. The range cannot widen mid-tune (PLAN rule
  7c: the same surface).
- **Owner:** B.3.

### 2. The shallower re-search fires only on bound echoes, and the tune turns it off (implementation units / cohesion)

- **Evidence.**
  - `CoreResearchShallower` runs 2 → 0, the bottom of its range, and 0 is
    off. The rule is `score < best_score + research_shallower`
    (`search_kernel_core.cpp` 1163) after a reduced search returned
    `score > alpha`, with `best_score ≤ alpha`.
  - Scores are integers. At 2 the rule fires only when `score == alpha + 1`
    and `best_score == alpha`: an earlier sibling returned exactly alpha,
    and the reduced search returned exactly beta at a null window.
  - Both are the bound echoes of the fail-hard quiescence, which returns
    `alpha` (lines 225 and 321), not the donor's "barely beat the best".
  - At 0 the rule can never fire.
- **A unit note.** The donor's `+8` converts to 2.26 (×0.282). In integer
  centipawns, "below 2.26" is a difference of 1 or 2. The seed 2 keeps only
  1, so rule 3's rounding halved the population before any tune.
- **Reading.** In this engine the mechanism tests score coincidences made by
  fail-hard bounds, not near-misses. The donor's test assumes fail-soft
  values everywhere.
- **Fix.** Bake 0 (off). Re-test the mechanism when B.4 makes quiescence
  fail-soft (the B.4 card already lists its fail-high blend), with the
  threshold at 3, the conversion rounded to cover the donor's range. If it
  is still off after B.4, B.8 deletes it.
- **Owner:** B.4 (re-test), B.8 (removal).

### 3. RFP is tuned toward more pruning on every term: the seed column was conservative (seed rule, not a defect)

- **Evidence.** Every RFP coordinate moved toward pruning more:
  - `CoreRfpPerPly` 133 → 113;
  - `CoreRfpImproving` 89 → 99;
  - `CoreRfpWorsening` 11 → 14;
  - `CoreRfpDepthCap` 9 → 10;
  - `CoreRfpBetaWeight` 661 → 682 (returns closer to beta);
  - `CoreRfpCorrMult` 676 → 581.

  No two of them cancel. The sweep re-run read WAC best at 0.5× on
  `CoreRfpPerPly` (packet §28).
- **Reading.** §7.2 rule 2 seeded the margin at the geometric mean of the
  oracle-converted and Basilisk-fitted columns. That was sized for B.0's
  92 cp search–static residual (§2.4). Cluster 1's correction history and
  TT-refined estimate shrink that residual, so the margin can tighten.
- **Fix.** None in B.2. C's evaluation programme re-fits RFP after any
  evaluation change, since the margin depends on the residual. At B.9, read
  rule 2 against all clusters' fitted margins.
- **Owner:** C, B.9.

### 4. All `cutoffCnt` LMR terms fall together (cohesion watch)

- **Evidence.** The three terms all fall:
  - `CoreLmrCutoffCnt` 264 → 243;
  - `CoreLmrCutoffCnt2` 1095 → 1030;
  - `CoreLmrCutoffCntAll` 1138 → 982 (a full step).
- **Reading.** Less extra reduction when the children keep failing high,
  most at all nodes. The all-node label is still partly the legacy one: the

- **Fix.** Re-read these terms after B.3 adopts the donor's typing there.
- **Owner:** B.3.

### 5. The correction learns faster (registered question)

- **Evidence.** `CoreCorrUpdateScale` runs 12 → 14 (a full step).
- **Reading.** Consistent with §24.1's registered `CoreCorrLimit` question:
  Basilisk's correction tables saturate at about 3.5× the donor's relative
  error, and the update arrives in Basilisk's evaluation units.
- **Fix.** The registered categorical run, `CoreCorrLimit` 1024 against
  289, if B.2.2's screens or the bake's readings show saturated positions
  carrying the tree.
- **Owner:** B.2.3 residual, as registered.

### Findings, final

1. **IIR stays tuned off (design / cohesion; owner B.3).**
   - `CoreIirMinDepth` holds 8, the top of its range, through block 2.
   - Finding 1 above stands: a fourth reduction for a missing TT move, on
     top of the donor's three.
   - B.3 replaces the legacy IIR with the donor's form and settles an
     IIR-off arm.
2. **The shallower re-search is off at theta too (implementation units /
   cohesion; owners B.4, B.8).**
   - Block 2 moved `CoreResearchShallower` from 0 to 1, but 1 is off as
     well. A re-searched move has `score ≥ alpha + 1 > best_score`, so
     `score < best_score + 1` never holds.
   - Any value ≤ 1 disables the rule, so the coordinate wandered inside a
     dead zone with no gradient. The bake takes 1, which is off.
   - Finding 2's fix stands: re-test after B.4's fail-soft quiescence with
     the threshold at 3, and delete it in B.8 if still unwanted.
3. **RFP prunes harder (seed rule, not a defect; owners C, B.9).**
   - `CoreRfpPerPly` 133 → 101 (−2.67 steps, the largest move of the tune);
   - `CoreRfpImproving` 89 → 98, `CoreRfpWorsening` 11 → 13,
     `CoreRfpDepthCap` 9 → 10, `CoreRfpCorrMult` 676 → 538;
   - `CoreRfpBetaWeight` returned to 661.
   - Finding 3 stands: rule 2's geometric-mean seed was sized for B.0's
     92 cp residual, and Cluster 1's correction shrinks it. C re-fits RFP
     after evaluation changes.
4. **Correction wants more weight and faster learning (open; owner B.2.3
   residual, decided at B.3).**
   - `CoreCorrUpdateScale` 12 → 16 (+2 steps);
   - `CoreCorrContWeight` 8761 → 9649 (+1 step).
   - This reverses the interim reading. The registered `CoreCorrLimit`
     question (§24.1) asks whether Basilisk's tables saturate too late;
     the fit says the correction is too weak or too slow, not too strong.
   - Running `CoreCorrLimit` 289 now would cap a signal the fit wants
     larger. That categorical is therefore not proposed on this evidence.
     The question to research is whether the correction update should be
     converted like rule 4: the update arrives in Basilisk's evaluation
     units while the table keeps the donor's limit.
5. **Histories weigh more (cohesion watch; owner B.2.3 residual).** Bonuses
   rise, the malus falls, and history's influence grows in two consumers:
   - `CoreQuietBonusSlope` 133 → 151;
   - `CoreTtCutoffBonusSlope` 131 → 151;
   - `CoreMalusSlope` 968 → 829;
   - `CoreLmrStatScale` 439 → 526 (history counts more in LMR);
   - `CoreCaptFutHistDiv` 27 → 15 (capture history counts more in
     capture futility).

   No pair cancels; they shift the same way. Two candidate causes:
   - the tables clear to 0 rather than the donor's negative fills
     (Amendment 1, decision 7);
   - fewer updates per move in a smaller tree.

   Decision 7 already names the categorical `CoreHistoryFills` as a
   B.2.3 residual. This is its first supporting evidence; it is not run
   before the gates.
6. **The `cutoffCnt` all-node term keeps falling (cohesion watch; owner
   B.3).** `CoreLmrCutoffCntAll` 1138 → 910 (−1.46 steps). Finding 4 stands:
   re-read it after B.3 adopts the donor's node typing for the null-move,
   ProbCut and singular children.
7. **The re-search deepens more readily (consistent, no action).**
   `CoreResearchDeeper` 15 → 13 (−1 step), the companion of the disabled
   shallower rule.
